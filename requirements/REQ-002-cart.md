# REQ-002 — Shopping cart

As a logged-in customer, I want to add and remove products from my cart so that I can review what I am about to buy.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-002.1** — Adding a product from the catalog increases the cart count shown in the header.
- **AC-002.2** — Removing a product from the catalog decreases the cart count; the count is hidden when the cart is empty.
- **AC-002.3** — The cart page lists each added product with its price and shows the total of all items.
- **AC-002.4** — An empty cart shows "Your cart is empty." and checkout is disabled.
- **AC-002.5** — The cart keeps its items while the customer moves between the catalog and the cart during the same session.
